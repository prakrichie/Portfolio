The pivot

After showing the product and receiving feedback, a quote that encouraged me to change my design decision was “don’t be safe and take a risk”. I took this onboard and decided to change and update my previous principles from a elegant, clean, and appearance.

The key takeaways were, make the user go

  • have a laugh
  • stress relief
  • what is going on?
  • question the design and developer
  • have a go at it

This leaves an impression and add onto my principle of giving the user a buffer for applying for positions

I thought to myself, what can make a user go “what the..” so I came up with this:

The weapon design
